# Models & XAI

## Failure Classification: benchmarked, not assumed

`ml/training/train_classifier.py` trains two candidates on the same held-out split and compares them by **PR-AUC** (`average_precision_score`) — the appropriate metric here since `failure_imminent` is a rare-event label, where ROC-AUC and accuracy can look good even when precision at low recall is poor:

- A plain `LogisticRegression` baseline (`class_weight='balanced'`).
- An Optuna-tuned `RandomForestClassifier`.

Whichever scores higher on PR-AUC is saved to `models/classifier.pkl` along with a tag naming the winning algorithm. On this synthetic dataset, `LogisticRegression` (~0.997 PR-AUC) has consistently beaten the tuned `RandomForestClassifier` (~0.991–0.994 PR-AUC) — the failure boundary in the synthetic data is close to linearly separable, so the extra complexity of a random forest isn't earning its keep here. The pipeline reports that honestly rather than hard-coding RandomForest as the "sophisticated" headline model.

`backend/services/predictor.py` reads the saved tag and loads whichever model actually won — it does not assume RandomForest.

## Remaining Useful Life (RUL)

An Optuna-tuned `RandomForestRegressor` (`ml/training/train_rul.py`) predicts cycles-to-failure directly.

## Anomaly Detection

An `IsolationForest` (`ml/training/train_anomaly.py`) flags sensor readings that don't resemble the training distribution.

## Explainable AI (SHAP)

`backend/services/xai.py` picks the SHAP explainer that matches the deployed classifier rather than assuming a tree model:

- `shap.TreeExplainer` when `RandomForestClassifier` won.
- `shap.LinearExplainer` (using a small stored background sample) when `LogisticRegression` won.

The same pattern is used in `evaluation/eval_shap.py` so the evaluation report always explains the model that's actually deployed.

## Data & Validation

All training and evaluation data is synthetically generated by `scripts/generate_dataset.py` — parametric noise plus a hand-authored non-linear degradation curve, not measurements from a physical machine. Reported metrics describe how well the models fit the synthetic generator's assumptions, not real-world failure signatures. See [evaluation/results/FINAL_REPORT.md](https://github.com/nithya-prakash/PredictiveMaintenanceAI/blob/main/evaluation/results/FINAL_REPORT.md) in the repo for the full benchmark (classification, RUL, cross-validation, latency, SHAP) generated by `evaluation/run_all.py`.
